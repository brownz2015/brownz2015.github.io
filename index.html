About

Social life is increasingly networked.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Sociology at the University of Washington, Seattle, where I study social networks, political sociology, social movements, and social media. I expect to complete my degree in Spring 2027.

My work uses computational methods to understand political communication, online extremism and disinformation, and the ways social groups form, endure, and change.

Research

Networks, political communities, and digital traces.

01

Online political communities

I examine network resilience, movement identity, and political diffusion in communities such as QAnon, r/The_Donald, Rumble, and Telegram.

02

Computational social science

My research combines network analysis, computational text analysis, digital-trace data, and applied quantitative methods.

03

Teaching & mentoring

I teach Data and Society and have supported students across sociology, criminology, statistics, theory, and related fields.
