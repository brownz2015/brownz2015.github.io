About

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Sociology at the University of Washington, Seattle, where I study social networks, political sociology, social movements, and social media. I expect to complete my degree in Spring 2027.

My work uses computational methods to understand political communication, online extremism and disinformation, and the ways social groups form, endure, and change.

Evaluating Quarantine as Platform Governance: Network Evidence of Structural Bifurcation in r/The_Donald

Uses network evidence to evaluate whether Reddit’s quarantine of r/The_Donald produced a meaningful structural rupture in the community.

02 · Under review

When Counterspeech Comes from Inside: Audience Discourse and Norm Stabilization on Rumble

Examines how audience-led counterspeech can shape discourse and stabilize social norms within a right-wing video platform.

03 · Revise & resubmit

Beyond Published Tables: Generating and Validating Custom Census Tract Demographics for Policy Analysis

Presents a method for producing and validating custom census-tract demographic estimates for policy analysis beyond published tables.

04 · Revise & resubmit

Sentiment Analysis at Scale: Tracking Russian Elite Trajectories through Text

Uses large-scale text analysis to track media sentiment toward Russian elites and relate it to changes in their political trajectories.

05 · In progress

Automated Tracking of Elite Interaction Networks in Authoritarian Regimes

Introduces an automated approach for mapping and following interaction networks among elites in authoritarian regimes.

06 · Dissertation

Post-January 6 QAnon: Network Resilience, Movement Identity, and Political Diffusion

Uses Telegram forwarding data and computational text analysis to trace QAnon’s resilience after January 6 and the diffusion of its claims into American political discourse.

07 · In progress

Contested Governance and Electoral Violence

Develops a computational framework for studying how rebel groups, criminal gangs, and separatist organizations pursue institutional legitimacy and local governance.
